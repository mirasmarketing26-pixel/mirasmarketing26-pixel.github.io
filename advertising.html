What we’ll need from you.

A clear goal, a budget you set and your sign-off on the ads.

  1. Your goal

  2. A monthly budget

    You decide what to spend; we suggest how to split it across the platforms.

  3. Access

    Access to your ad accounts and website, or your go-ahead to open new accounts.

  4. One person to approve

    Someone on your side who signs off the ads before they go live.
